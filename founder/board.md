# Board summary — Wibe

## Vote

**3 FUND IF, 0 FUND, 0 PASS** · Average score **۴٫۷ / ۱۰** (Offers ۵, Monopoly ۴, Product ۵)

No outright pass and no unconditional fund. The board agrees the pain and the shipped product are real; the business is not yet proven as a durable, monetizable destination.

## Risks more than one member raised

These go first — they appeared across lenses:

1. **Paying side is unclear while users pay zero.** Offers and Monopoly both treat ads/sponsors/outbound links as the real market. If that inventory never clears a floor that funds curation, user love is not a company. (CFO / pricing later.)
2. **Seven categories = no beachhead / no “one thing.”** Monopoly and Product both kill horizontal sprawl: thin everywhere, first nowhere; home screen as directory, not a finished job.
3. **“Curated and kept up to date” is the whole claim — and easy to fail.** Offers (likelihood), Product (first five minutes), and Monopoly (10× vs Instagram/Telegram) all put freshness, craft, and proof above slogans. Stale or thin lists kill return visits and thus ad inventory.
4. **Easy substitutes, weak moat.** Instagram saves, Telegram channels, blogs, copycat list sites. Offers calls commodity free lists; Monopoly calls no durable advantage. Without a falsifiable 10× and a compounding editorial loop, this is a crowded free-content fight.
5. **Monetization can poison trust.** Product alone stresses seams (sponsor/affiliate louder than taste); Offers asks whether paid buyers feel stupid saying no vs Instagram/Telegram sponsorship. Same underlying risk: money path vs editorial promise.

## Disagreements (kept on purpose)

- **Monopoly is harshest on structure (۴):** wants monopoly of a *small* market and a named secret; treats breadth as the strategic error.
- **Offers is harshest on the unpaid→paid bridge (۵):** will fund if the *payer* offer is written and the free value equation stays proven — less obsessed with cutting categories except as a wedge for money + pain.
- **Product is harshest on experience focus (۵):** will fund if one job owns the first five minutes and monetization cannot override craft — less about “market monopoly,” more about saying no inside the product.

A board that agreed on everything would not be doing its job.

## Conditions checklist (merged)

For the rest of the founder pack to tick:

- [ ] **Beachhead:** Pick one primary vertical (or one tight pair with the same use moment); park the rest as “later,” not equal-weight nav. Prove ownership of that small market before expanding. → `/founder-competitors`, `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] **Paid offer first:** Name first paying customer type, unit sold (placement / list sponsor / outbound click / package), target price band, and why they buy Wibe over Instagram ads or Telegram sponsorship — no invented CPMs. → `/founder-pricing`, `/founder-cfo`, `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] **Freshness SLA + craft bar:** Define what “up to date” means per list type, show it in product, and what users see when a list slips; non-negotiable ship standard (depth, recency, voice). → ops + `/founder-consumer` for retention proof
- [ ] **Editorial rules monetization cannot override:** How sponsors and outbound links appear, when refused, how the list still feels curated after the click. → brand / ops / offer
- [ ] **10× / secret in writing:** Falsifiable test vs Instagram/Telegram DIY in five minutes (freshness, selection, structure, trust, or speed). If neither secret nor 10× exists, do not scale spend. → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] **First-five-minutes job:** One sentence outcome; home path only for that job; prove with real (or panel) users before scope expands. → `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] **Distribution without hope:** Repeatable path to first 100 then weekly habit; rough CAC / effort-to-habit. → `/founder-consumer`, `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] **Moat design:** Suggestion → curation loop (or other proprietary process) that compounds so month-six copycats face more than cloned pages. → ops / product
- [ ] **Unit economics floor:** Minimum engagement / outbound metric at which ads or sponsor packages fund curation labor. → `/founder-cfo`

## Strongest version of this business the board can see

This differs from the pitch of “seven categories of free curated lists, monetize somehow.”

**Wibe as the default Persian destination for one high-intent job** — e.g. “what should I watch tonight” *or* “where do I eat / go this weekend” — where editorial lists are visibly fresher and better structured than Instagram saves and Telegram dumps, users return on habit, and sponsors/outbound partners buy *that* concentrated attention under rules that never outrank taste. Only after that slice is owned do the other categories earn a seat. The product being ready is an advantage *if* it is used to prove the wedge and the payer offer — not to defend a horizontal portal that is free forever and funded by unspecified ads.
