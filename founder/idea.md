# Wibe (ویب)

- What it is: A Persian web app of curated, kept-up-to-date lists across film, series, books, podcasts, travel, restaurants and cafés, so people can use ready-made collections instead of endlessly building and sorting their own from Instagram and other sources; users can also suggest items to add.
- Who it is for: People in Iran who want tidy, collected, regularly updated lists they can use fast — without having to assemble and sort lists themselves from Instagram and scattered sources.
- What it sells, at what price: Free for end users; no direct pay-from-user model. Intended revenue: ads, sponsorships, and other paths not fully decided yet (e.g. outbound links from items to shops and web pages). Core “product” is the curated lists and discovery experience.
- Where and how: Online (website / web app), Persian (Farsi) and RTL, for users in Iran. Delivery is digital: browse lists by category, open items, suggest additions. Discovery expected via content, social, and search over time.
- Budget and constraints: Product is already built and ready. Monetization path is open (ads / sponsors / affiliate-style links). Do not invent market size or competitor set here.
